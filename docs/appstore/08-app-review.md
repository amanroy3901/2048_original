## Review notes (paste into "Notes", 4000 max)

```
Thank you for reviewing 2048 Elemental Sprites.

SIGN-IN: None. The app has no accounts, no login and no server-side user data. All progress is stored on the device.

PURCHASES AND ADS: None. The app is free with no in-app purchases and no ads. "Coins" are earned only by playing (merges, medals, the daily challenge) and spent only on power-ups. They cannot be bought.

HOW TO REACH EVERY FEATURE (from the home screen):
- Classic: tap PLAY on "Classic". Swipe to merge matching sprites. Power-ups (Undo, Hammer, Shuffle, Freeze, Hint) are at the bottom.
- Time Attack: tap PLAY on "Time Attack". A 60-second round; big merges add time.
- Shooter: tap PLAY on "Shooter". Tap a lane to launch the next sprite upward; matching sprites merge.
- Daily challenge: the "Daily" card on the home screen (it appears from the first launch of each day).
- Medals: the "Medals" button at the bottom of the home screen.
- Profile and settings: the gear icon at the top right. Sound, vibration and the two privacy switches are here.

PRIVACY AND CONSENT:
- Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) are on by default and can be turned off in Profile > Privacy > "Share crash reports".
- Gameplay statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) are OFF by default. After the player's first game, a one-time card on the home screen asks "Help Blaze get better!" with "Allow" and "Not now". Both answers are saved, the card never appears again, and the choice can be changed in Profile > Privacy > "Share gameplay stats".
- To see the card: play any one game (start Classic, swipe once, return to the home screen).
- No tracking: the app does not use the advertising identifier (AdSupport is not linked), so it does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

PERMISSIONS: The app requests no system permissions (no camera, location, contacts, photos, notifications or tracking).

The app works fully offline.
```
