Privacy Policy

2048 Elemental Sprites for iPhone and iPad · Sayne Design · Last updated: November 5, 2026

2048 Elemental Sprites is a puzzle game. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no in-app purchases, and it plays offline. This policy explains the small amount of data the app can send, and how you control it.

What stays on your device

Your saved game, scores, coins, medals, daily challenge progress and settings are stored only on your device. We can't see them. They are deleted when you delete the app.

Crash reports (on by default)

If the game crashes, it sends a crash report through Firebase Crashlytics (Google) so we can find and fix the problem. A report contains the technical details of the crash (stack trace, device model, iOS version, app version, free memory and disk), a random installation identifier, short notes about what the game was doing (for example "started a Classic game"), and app session information used to count crash-free sessions.

You can turn this off at any time: Profile → Privacy → Share crash reports.

Gameplay statistics (only if you allow them)

After your first game, the app asks once whether you'll share anonymous gameplay statistics. Nothing is sent unless you tap Allow. If you allow it, Google Analytics for Firebase receives events such as which mode you started, which power-up you used, medals unlocked and daily challenges completed, together with screen views, session times, a random app-instance identifier, device and iOS version, and an approximate country or region worked out from your IP address. Google does not give us the IP address itself.

You can change your answer at any time: Profile → Privacy → Share gameplay stats.

What we never collect

  • No name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or messages
  • No precise location
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA), and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No payment information

The identifiers above are random. They are not linked to your identity, and we do not combine them with other data to find out who you are.

We don't sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except Google, which processes it for us as a service provider under its own terms: Firebase privacy and security and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed specifically at children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Gameplay statistics are off unless someone taps Allow, and crash reports can be turned off in Profile.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the DPDP Act in India), you may have the right to access, correct or delete data about you, or to object to its use. Because we don't link data to your identity, the simplest way to stop all collection is to turn both switches off in Profile, or delete the app. For any request, email us and include the approximate date you used the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
