2048 Elemental Sprites: Support

For iPhone and iPad · Sayne Design

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email aman.kroy2001@gmail.com. Tell us your device and iOS version, and we usually reply within two working days.

How do I play?

Swipe to slide every sprite. Two matching sprites merge into the next element. Keep merging to reach 2048 and the Ember King. Tap Help in a game for the tutorial.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your device automatically. There's no account or cloud save, so deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I earn coins?

By playing: merges, medals and the daily challenge pay coins. Coins buy power-ups. There are no purchases in the game.

How do I turn off crash reports or gameplay stats?

Open the gear icon → Privacy, and switch off "Share crash reports" or "Share gameplay stats". See the privacy policy.

Sound or vibration

Gear icon → Sound / Vibration.
