**Full description:**

Your 2048 tiles have come alive!

Every number is now a sprite that grows up as you merge: little Drip the water drop becomes Sprig, Sunny, Frosty, Orbi, Blaze and on up to the legendary Ember King at 2048. Slide twins together and watch them evolve with a flash, a swirl and a shower of sparkles.

THE SPRITES ARE ALIVE
• They blink, breathe and look at their twin when a merge is close
• Poke one and it giggles (poke too often and it gets grumpy)
• Leave them alone and they start thinking, then fall asleep
• Get stuck and they worry; bump a wall and everyone goes dizzy

THREE WAYS TO PLAY
• Classic: the 2048 you love on 3×3, 4×4, 5×5 or 6×6 boards
• Time Attack: one minute on the clock, and big merges buy more time
• Neon Shooter: launch sprites up five lanes and chain the merges

SOMETHING NEW EVERY DAY
• A daily challenge with coins and a streak bonus
• 24 medals to earn across all modes
• Collect all 11 sprites
• Power-ups: Undo, Hammer, Shuffle, Freeze and Hint

Sign in with Google to keep your scores, coins, medals and collection safe on any device. Plays offline.

## Before publishing

1. **Deploy the Firestore rules** (adds `player_progress/{uid}`, owner-only, with field and size checks).
   Without this, cloud sync of progress quietly does nothing; the game is unaffected.
   ```
   cd 2048_original && firebase deploy --only firestore:rules --project original-64f6c
   ```
2. Build a signed release (`./gradlew :app:bundleRelease`) and test it on a real mid-range phone:
   cold start, a long Classic game, a Neon Shooter chain, Time Attack to time-up.
   Release builds now use R8 (code shrinking and obfuscation) and resource shrinking, which cut the APK from
   19.2 MB to 5.0 MB. Keep rules for Gson, the Firestore model and Credential Manager are in `app/proguard-rules.pro`.
   The Crashlytics plugin uploads the R8 mapping file with each release build, so crash reports stay readable.
   Check Google sign-in on the release build: the Play app-signing SHA-1 must be registered in Firebase.
3. Update the listing name, descriptions, icon, feature graphic and screenshots in Play Console.
4. Roll out to a small percentage first and watch Crashlytics and the new analytics events.

## Analytics events added

`achievement_unlocked` (achievement_id, tier) · `daily_challenge_done` (kind, target) ·
`daily_challenge_claimed` (reward, streak) · `elemental_welcome_seen` · plus the existing
`power_up_used`, `shooter_level_up`.

  Scores, the saved game and coins carry over.
