Delete your account

You can ask us to delete your 2048 Elemental Sprites account and everything saved in the cloud at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Email aman.kroy2001@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my 2048 account”.
  2. Send it from, or mention, the Google account email you signed in with.
  3. We confirm by email and delete your account and cloud data within 30 days.
Email a deletion request

What's deleted

  • Your sign-in record: Google account ID, name and email
  • Your cloud save: scores, level and XP, coins, medals and collected sprites

What's kept

  • Anonymous analytics and crash reports, not linked to your name or email, deleted automatically within 14 months
  • Game data stored only on your phone, until you clear the app's data or uninstall

Just want to stop syncing?

Sign out any time from the Profile screen in the game. Everything keeps working offline without an account.
