What we collect

If you just play, your game stays on your phone. A few things leave the device to keep the game working and help us fix bugs:

Google sign-in
Optional		Your Google account ID, name and email address, used to create your game account.
Cloud save
Optional		Scores, level and XP, coins, medals and the sprites you've collected, so your progress follows you to a new phone.
App analytics
Firebase Analytics		How the game is used (screens opened, games played), device and app info, and identifiers including the Android advertising ID. Used only to improve the game, never for ads.
Crash reports
Crashlytics		Crash logs and diagnostics about your device and the app, used to find and fix bugs.
On your phone onlyYour saved game, best scores and settings. They never leave the device unless you sign in.

What we don't collect

  • Location, contacts, photos, videos or files
  • Messages, microphone or camera
  • Payment or financial information

Who handles your data

Your data is processed for us by Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics and Crashlytics), under Firebase's privacy terms.

  • Everything is encrypted in transit.
  • Your cloud save can only be read or changed by your own signed-in account.
  • We don't share, rent or sell your data to anyone else.

Keeping & deleting

  • Account and cloud save data are kept until you ask us to delete them.
  • Analytics data is deleted automatically after at most 14 months.
  • You can sign out any time from the Profile screen, clear the app's data, or uninstall.

Age

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child has signed in, tell us and we'll delete their data.
